A note from our founder

Message from the Director & Founder

Welcome to ICT Professional Training Center.

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to our training center. Since our establishment in 2019, ICT Professional Training Center has been committed to providing high-quality IT education and practical skills that meet the demands of today's digital world.

Technology continues to transform the way we live, work, and communicate. Therefore, our mission is not only to teach technical knowledge but also to empower students with problem-solving abilities, creativity, professionalism, and confidence to succeed in their careers.

At ICT Professional Training Center, we offer industry-relevant short courses including C++, Java Programming, Web Development, Cybersecurity, Data Analysis, Digital Marketing, Power BI, Graphic Design, and many other technology-focused programs. Our courses are designed to combine theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical experience, ensuring that students are ready for real-world challenges.

We believe that education is the foundation of personal and professional growth. Whether you are a student, job seeker, working professional, or business owner, our training programs are designed to help you achieve your goals and unlock new opportunities.

Thank you for choosing ICT Professional Training Center as your learning partner. We look forward to supporting your journey toward success in the ever-evolving world of technology.

NN
Mr. Nhanh Nhim Director & Founder of ICT Professional Training Center

A timeline

Our Journey

The journey of ICT Professional Training Center from its establishment to becoming a trusted destination for ICT education.

2019

Our Beginning

ICT Professional Training Center was established in 2019 with a vision to provide practical and professional ICT education for students, graduates, and working professionals.

2020

Bridging the Skills Gap

Recognizing the growing demand for digital skills and technology expertise, the center expanded to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry requirements.

2021

Expanding the Curriculum

New programs in Web Development, Data Analysis, and Digital Marketing were introduced, giving students more paths into the tech industry.

2022

Growing Community

Our student community passed the 1,000 mark, supported by a growing team of industry instructors and mentors.

2024

Regional Recognition

Recognized as a trusted ICT training provider, with graduates placed in leading tech companies across the region.

2026

Today

3,000+ graduates and 12+ active courses later, we keep building the same thing we started with: practical skills, taught well.
